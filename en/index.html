FAQ

Questions you might have

I know nothing about AI. Can I still work with you?

Yes, and you're very welcome. Consultations start from your work and daily life. No technical background is needed, and we avoid jargon.

Do I need to spend a lot on tools?

Not necessarily. Many needs can be met with free or low-cost monthly tools. We confirm your needs first, then recommend the most cost-effective approach.

Will AI replace me or my staff?

Our approach is to let AI handle the repetitive, time-consuming parts and leave work that needs experience and judgment to people. The goal is to give people more breathing room.

Is it safe to give company data to AI?

It depends on the tools and settings. During planning we agree together on which data can and can't be used, and choose options that meet your requirements.

I already have a website. Why do I need AI search visibility?

If your site only has a basic company introduction, AI knows you exist but not which questions you're the right answer for. Clear services, case studies and FAQs, plus structured data, give AI something it can cite.

How soon will I see results?

It depends on the scope. Individuals usually notice a difference within days. For team workflows, we typically start with a small pilot before deciding whether to scale up.
